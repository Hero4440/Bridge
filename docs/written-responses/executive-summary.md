# Executive summary

Bridge is an AI social coach for neurodivergent young adults (18–30) who want connection but find social moments overwhelming. Starting a conversation, reading tone, staying calm, and moving from chatting online to meeting in person all demand executive function. When that load is too high, people avoid it and become isolated.

With Bridge, users practice real conversations with Mo, a calm AI buddy who plays the other person and gives kind feedback. Support fades from choices to hints to the user's own words. Users then put those skills to work with real people: interest circles and events for meeting new people, and "My people" for staying close to family, roommates and coworkers. A daily check-in asks what each person wants help with, so Bridge adapts to their own goals.

Safety is built in: a tone check before sending, a calm-down space, unsafe-message warnings, and a one-tap "I need help" that reaches a trusted person or 988. Mo never speaks for the user. It suggests, and the user decides.

Our impact goal is more conversations started, more events attended, and more confidence, with users relying on Mo less over time.
