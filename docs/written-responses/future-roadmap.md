# Future roadmap

**Next 3 months.** Run a 4–6 week pilot with First Place AZ residents and staff, measuring conversations started, events attended and self-rated confidence. Mo already runs on Claude through Amazon Bedrock for practice feedback, tone checks and unsafe-message detection. Next we test that feedback with neurodivergent users and strengthen safety filters and human escalation.

**Limitations we're addressing.**
- AI feedback can be wrong or feel judgmental, so Mo explains its reasoning, users can ignore any suggestion, and we will test tone feedback with neurodivergent users.
- Meeting in person carries risk, so we plan verified members, moderation, and check-ins before and after events.
- Our prototype hasn't yet been tested with residents, which is the pilot's first goal.

**Scaling.** Co-design accessibility features (read-aloud, voice input, plain-language mode), then expand to supported-living programs, vocational services and college disability offices through program licenses. The app stays free for individuals, so people without a staffed community can still use it.
